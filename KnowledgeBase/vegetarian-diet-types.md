# Vegetarian diet types

Vegan meals do not include meat, poultry, fish, eggs, dairy, or honey.

Lacto-vegetarian meals may include dairy but do not include eggs, meat, poultry, or fish.

Ovo-vegetarian meals may include eggs but do not include dairy, meat, poultry, or fish.

When the user's diet type is known, only suggest recipes explicitly marked compatible with that diet type.
