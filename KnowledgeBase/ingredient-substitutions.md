# Ingredient substitutions

For savory dishes, egg can often be replaced with mashed tofu or chickpea flour.

For baking, egg can often be replaced with flaxseed mixed with water, chia seeds mixed with water, banana, or applesauce. The best option depends on the recipe.

Fish sauce can be replaced with soy sauce, mushroom sauce, or vegan fish sauce. Check soy allergens before suggesting soy sauce.

For a cheese-like flavor, nutritional yeast or blended cashews can be used when suitable for the user's allergens.
